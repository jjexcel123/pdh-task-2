Direct Quotes from The Promise of Adolescence

Quotes and content are directly copied from:
"The Promise of Adolescence: Realizing Opportunity for All Youth" (National Academies Press, 2019).
“Adolescence is a period of significant brain development that contributes to young people’s ability to make decisions, solve problems, and control their impulses.”
- The Promise of Adolescence, Chapter 3, National Academies Press, 2019
“Changes in the brain’s dopamine system during adolescence may increase sensitivity to rewards and contribute to increased risk-taking behavior.”
- The Promise of Adolescence, Chapter 3, National Academies Press, 2019
“While cognitive capacity reaches adult levels by mid-adolescence, the ability to employ mature self-regulation under emotionally arousing circumstances lags behind.”
- The Promise of Adolescence, Chapter 3, National Academies Press, 2019
“Because the development of the reward system and the cognitive control system are not synchronized, adolescents are particularly susceptible to engaging in risky and impulsive behaviors.”
- The Promise of Adolescence, Chapter 3, National Academies Press, 2019
“Adolescence is characterized by rapid change—not only physically, but also emotionally, cognitively, and socially.”
- The Promise of Adolescence, Executive Summary, National Academies Press, 2019
“Increasing evidence shows that the way adolescents think and make decisions is different from adults, especially in social and emotional contexts.”
- The Promise of Adolescence, Chapter 3, National Academies Press, 2019
“Youth are capable of significant resilience and positive change, particularly when given the appropriate supports and opportunities.”
- The Promise of Adolescence, Preface, National Academies Press, 2019
